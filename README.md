# Blinkit Store Data Analysis | Interactive Dashboard (Power BI & MS Excel)
## Project Objective
Blinkit Store wanted to create an annual sales report to analyze sales performance, identify growth and decline patterns, and understand customer behavior. The goal is to use these insights to improve sales strategy and drive business growth in upcoming years.

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/yudhistersahu966/DATA-ANALYSIS-DASHBOARD/blob/main/BlinkIT%20Grocery%20Data.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)

- What is the total sales revenue and total number of orders?

- How do sales and orders trend over time?

- Which month recorded the highest sales and orders?

- Which product categories contribute the most to revenue?

- What is the outlet-wise sales distribution?

- Which items are the top-selling products?

- What is the average order value (AOV)?

- Which outlets and categories show sales growth or decline?

- What percentage of sales comes from each category and outlet type?

- How does customer demand vary across product segments?

## Project Insights

- Grocery and daily essentials contribute the highest share of total sales

- Certain outlet types generate maximum revenue consistently

- Monthly sales trends show clear seasonal demand patterns

- A small set of products contributes to a large portion of overall revenue

- Average order value remains stable, indicating consistent customer purchasing behavior

## Final Conclusion

The analysis highlights strong demand for essential grocery items and high-performing outlets that drive most of Blinkit’s revenue. By focusing on top-selling categories, optimizing inventory for high-demand products, and strengthening high-performing outlets, Blinkit can improve operational efficiency and increase overall sales growth through data-driven decision-making.
